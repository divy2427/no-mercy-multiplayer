THE TABLE RULES

Friends first.
Until the first +4.

Create a private room and share the code. Once 4–6 players have joined, the host can deal.

  1. Match the top card’s color or value.
  2. Play a wild card to choose the next color.
  3. Can’t play? Draw one card and your turn ends.
  4. Empty your hand first to win.

+2 and +4 make the next player draw and miss a turn. Reverse changes direction. This prototype uses basic Uno-style rules.
