SHOW NO MERCY

The table rules.

Deal 7 cards each. Match the color, number or symbol. Wild cards can be played on any color. The first discard is a number card.

  1. No match? Draw until you find a playable card, then play that card.
  2. Stacking: respond to a draw penalty with a draw card of equal or higher value, regardless of color. Otherwise take the full stack and miss your turn.
  3. Mercy: reach 25 cards and you are eliminated. Empty your hand or be the last player standing to win.
  4. 7: swap hands with a player you choose. 0: pass every hand in the direction of play.
  5. UNO: call at one card, or arm the button before playing from two. Other players can catch an uncalled one-card hand for a 2-card penalty until the next player acts.

Draw 2 / Draw 4: start or increase the draw stack. Skip: skip the next player. Reverse: change direction (take another turn with two players). Discard All: discard all cards of its color. Skip Everyone: take another turn.

Wild Reverse +4: reverse direction and start/increase the stack. With two players, the penalty comes back to YOU; stack it back or take it. Wild +6 / +10: stack the indicated penalty. Color Roulette: the next player chooses a color, draws until that color appears (wilds do not count), then misses their turn.

Optional scoring: the hand winner receives opponents’ number values, 20 per colored action, 50 per wild action, and 250 per eliminated player. First to 1000 wins the match.

This app extends the printed 2–6 player range to 2–8 as requested. Computer games run locally. Start the next hand to play again.
